## Quick Summary

| Aspect | Greenfield | Brownfield |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| **Definition** | Building a system from scratch with no constraints | Developing within or on top of existing systems |
| **Analogy** | Building on an empty green field | Building on a previously developed (brown) site |
| **Freedom** | Complete architectural freedom | Constrained by existing architecture |
| **Risk** | Higher upfront risk, uncertain requirements | Lower risk but higher complexity |
| **Cost** | High initial investment | Lower initial cost, ongoing maintenance |
| **Timeline** | Longer to first delivery | Faster incremental improvements |
| **Trending Because** | Cloud-native, microservices, digital transformation | Legacy modernization, technical debt, AI/ML integration |

## Why This Matters in 2024-2025

The IT industry is experiencing a massive shift:
- **$2.8 trillion** is spent annually on IT worldwide
- **70-80%** of enterprise IT budgets go toward maintaining existing (brownfield) systems
- **Digital transformation** initiatives are forcing organizations to decide: rebuild or modernize?
- **Cloud migration** has made greenfield approaches more accessible than ever
- **AI and automation** are enabling faster brownfield modernization
